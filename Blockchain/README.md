# Blockchain

This domain focuses on blockchain fundamentals and smart-contract development — from how Bitcoin works to writing and deploying your own contracts on Ethereum.

Work through the resources roughly in order: understand the fundamentals first, then move on to Solidity and full-stack Web3.

---

## Resources

### Prerequisite
- [How Bitcoin Works](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bBC-nXj3Ng4) – understand the original blockchain before building on one.

### Blockchain fundamentals
- [Blockchain Fundamentals](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLgPmWS2dQHW-BRQCQCNYgmHUfCN115pn0) – lectures 1–12 cover the core concepts.  
- [Ethereum Basics](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLgPmWS2dQHW9FmqNqug3M5ooNuRqP-alu) – videos 1–18 explain how Ethereum works.

### Smart contract development
- [Solidity Full Course](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLgPmWS2dQHW9u6IXZq5t5GMQTpW7JL33i) – learn Solidity, the language of Ethereum smart contracts.

### Full-stack Web3
- [Blockchain to Full-Stack Web3 Tutorial](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gyMwXuJrbJQ) – an all-in-one course taking you from basics to full dApps.
